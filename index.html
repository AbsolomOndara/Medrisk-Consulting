Before we begin

A few useful answers.

What happens in the first meeting?
We discuss the challenge, who it affects, what has already been tried and the outcome you need. It is a working conversation—not a sales presentation.
Do you tailor engagements to each organization?
Yes. Scope, delivery and support are shaped around your organization’s size, sector, people and resources.
Can Medrisk support implementation?
Yes. Depending on the assignment, we can move from diagnosis and design into training, implementation support and progress reviews.
Which sectors do you work with?
Our experience spans technology, retail, healthcare, hospitality, financial services, media, manufacturing and other growing organizations.
Where is Medrisk based?
We are based in Nairobi, Kenya, and work with organizations across Kenya and beyond.
